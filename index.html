Debate Arena

Two GPT Audio Mini agents exchange their actual generated audio directly. FOR builds a structured case; AGAINST challenges assumptions and evidence. All speakers use the same model with different roles and voices. Each must identify and answer the opponent’s strongest live claim before advancing a new argument. A closing round asks each what evidence or argument would change its mind. You are a third chair. Use Jump in by microphone or Jump in with text during the debate. Both agents receive microphone recordings as audio, while typed interjections and attachment text are sent as text. After each round, a neutral audio moderator states the live disagreement, names repetition fouls or evasions, and directs the next round toward the weakest unresolved point of the side behind on its burden of proof. These are model instructions, not guarantees of compliance. The first AI debater to say “I concede this debate” loses. Detection uses its generated transcript; your microphone audio is not transcribed, so your concession is not automatically detected. If neither AI concedes by the round limit, there is no winner.
