Debate Arena

Two GPT Audio agents exchange their actual generated audio directly. FOR builds a structured case; AGAINST challenges assumptions and evidence. Both are the same model with different roles and voices. Each is instructed to press the opponent’s weakest claim, demand a specific number, source or mechanism, and end with a direct question or burden, without padding or repeating earlier points. You are a third chair. Tap Jump in any time during the debate to record your voice. Both agents receive your recording as audio, not text. A neutral audio moderator checks recent audio after each AI turn and speaks only if it detects circular reasoning, repetition, or evasion. The first AI debater to say “I concede this debate” loses. Detection uses its generated transcript; your microphone audio is not transcribed, so your concession is not automatically detected. If neither AI concedes by the round limit, there is no winner.
